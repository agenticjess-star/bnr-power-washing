01The problem

Why pressure-washing a roof is a mistake

Asphalt shingles are sealed by mineral granules pressed into a base mat. High pressure blasts those granules off — shortening the roof's lifespan by years and voiding most manufacturer warranties. The black streaks you see are gloeocapsa magma, an algae that feeds on limestone in modern shingles. The right answer is the ARMA (Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association) recommended method — a soft wash that kills the algae and lets rain rinse it away over the next few weeks. That's what we do, and it's the only roof method we offer.

02The method

How a roof soft wash works

Different surface, different method. We never use one tool for everything — that's how good outdoor surfaces get damaged.

STEP 01

Pre-Inspection

Walk the roof line, identify problem areas, check gutter condition, photograph baseline. Tarp landscaping below, pre-rinse all plant beds.

STEP 02

ARMA-compliant Solution

Biodegradable sodium hypochlorite blend, mixed at recommended dilution, applied at less than 100 PSI from extension equipment — no foot traffic on shingles where possible, no high pressure ever.

STEP 03

Dwell + Verify

Solution dwells while we monitor for run-off control. Algae starts breaking down within minutes. We rinse strategically without rinsing off the protective treatment too quickly.

STEP 04

Gutter Service (optional add-on)

Hand-clear gutters of all debris, flush downspouts, brighten gutter exteriors with the same soft-wash mix. Most clients book this as a package — it's the natural pair-up.

05FAQ

What people ask, straight answers.

Will roof cleaning void my shingle warranty?+

Not when done with ARMA-compliant soft-wash methods, which is what we use. Pressure-washing a roof will void most warranties — that's not a method we offer for that exact reason.

How long do results last?+

In Houston's humid climate, expect 2–4 years before regrowth becomes visible. Adding a clear-coat preventive treatment (which we offer) extends that to 4–6 years.

Will the runoff hurt my landscaping?+

No. We pre-rinse every plant bed and post-rinse on the way out. The dilution is below the threshold that affects established plants, and our crew is trained to control flow paths.

Do you do tile roofs?+

Yes — clay and concrete tile both. The technique differs (more dwell time, less direct rinsing on the surface) but the soft-wash method is the same.

Do you handle the gutters too?+

Yes — gutter clearing (interior debris removal + downspout flush) is a popular add-on. Gutter brightening (removing the black streaks on the gutter face) is included with the roof wash itself.

How much warning do you need?+

Typically 3–7 days for residential, same-week for commercial. Weather-sensitive — we don't soft-wash a roof in the rain or with rain forecast within 4 hours of finish.
