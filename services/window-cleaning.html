01The problem

What makes Houston windows look wrong

Hard water mineral deposits are silica and calcium that bond to glass on exposure to UV and heat. They're not dirt — soap won't lift them. Sprinkler over-spray, pollen, gulf-breeze sea-salt, and screen-mesh dust compound the issue. The right approach is a two-stage process: a mineral-specific scrub to break the bond, then a squeegee-and-microfiber pass for streak-free finish. The frame, sill, and screen all get the same care — that's the difference between a 'window cleaning' and a 'window service.'

02The method

How we clean windows the right way

Different surface, different method. We never use one tool for everything — that's how good outdoor surfaces get damaged.

STEP 01

Pre-Audit

Walk the property, count windows, flag any with hard-water staining, broken seals, or stuck sashes. Confirm interior or interior-plus-exterior scope. Move blinds where needed.

STEP 02

Mineral Treatment (where needed)

On panes with hard-water staining, a mineral-bonding scrub breaks the silica/calcium attachment to the glass. This step is what separates a 'wash' from actually cleaning the window.

STEP 03

Squeegee & Microfiber Pass

Professional squeegee technique with overlapping passes — no streaks, no missed corners. Microfiber on edges, sill, and frame. Each pane gets full coverage including frame and sill.

STEP 04

Screen & Track

Screens hand-cleaned, tracks vacuumed and wiped. Reset screens to original position. Final inspection at exterior for streak-check.

05FAQ

What people ask, straight answers.

Do you do inside windows?+

Yes — inside, outside, or both. Interior cleaning includes the frame, sill, sash track, and screen. We move and reset blinds where needed.

Can you remove hard water spots?+

Most of them. Light to moderate spotting comes off completely with a mineral-specific treatment. Severely etched glass (10+ years of deposit) may keep a faint shadow — we'll be honest about that on the walk-through.

Do you clean high windows?+

Yes — up to 3 stories with extension equipment, higher with rope-access partner crews for commercial. We don't risk a ladder beyond what's safe.

Will you clean my screens?+

Yes — included in the price. We hand-clean, rinse, dry, and reset every screen on the windows we wash.

How often should I have my windows cleaned?+

Houston's pollen + humidity makes twice-yearly the sweet spot for most homes. Spring (post-pollen) and fall are the natural windows. Twice-yearly clients get a 20% recurring discount.

Do you use squeegees or those rotating tools?+

Squeegees — every time, by trained crew. Rotating spinners leave swirl marks on hard-water-stained panes. The squeegee technique is what gives you a true streak-free finish.
