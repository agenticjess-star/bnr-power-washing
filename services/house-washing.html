01The problem

What dirty siding actually costs you

Algae and mildew aren't just cosmetic. The biological growth feeds on the binder in your siding, oxidizes paint, and migrates into the substrate. Curb-appeal hits property valuations on the open market — appraisers and buyers both clock it instantly. And once mildew has been visible for two summers, the staining penetrates deep enough that ordinary cleaning won't lift it. Soft wash, done right, reverses all of it.

02The method

How we soft-wash Houston siding

Different surface, different method. We never use one tool for everything — that's how good outdoor surfaces get damaged.

STEP 01

Walk-through & Pre-rinse

Crew chief tarps landscaping, pre-rinses every adjacent plant bed, and flags any sensitive areas (gas meters, AC condensers, low-voltage lighting). Water-source, access, and shut-off plan confirmed.

STEP 02

Soft-wash Application

ARMA-compliant biodegradable solution applied at low pressure (under 500 PSI at the surface) — algae, mildew, oxidation, and surface dirt break their bond with the substrate. Dwell time is monitored by surface type.

STEP 03

Rinse & Detail

Top-down rinse with surface-appropriate pressure. Soffits, downspouts, gutter exteriors, and trim get attention. Crew member double-checks corner returns and behind shrubs.

STEP 04

Final walk-through

We don't pack up until you've walked the full perimeter with the crew chief and confirmed the result. If something needs another pass, we do it on the spot.

05FAQ

What people ask, straight answers.

How long does house washing take?+

Most single-family homes in Houston take 2–4 hours from setup to final walk-through. Larger or two-story properties can take a half-day. We schedule full days so we never rush.

Will it damage my plants or grass?+

No. We pre-rinse every plant bed adjacent to the wash zone, the solution is biodegradable and ARMA-compliant, and we post-rinse on the way out. We have not had a plant-damage claim in our service area.

How often should I have my house washed?+

In Houston's humid climate, once every 12–18 months is the sweet spot. Properties heavily shaded or surrounded by trees (algae loves shade) often go to once a year.

Do you do soft wash or pressure wash on siding?+

Soft wash, every time, on residential siding. Pressure wash on siding is how you damage paint, drive water behind seams, or void manufacturer warranties. The only thing pressure-washed on a residential job is concrete or masonry hardscape.

Are you insured?+

Yes — full general liability AND workers comp. We can email you a Certificate of Insurance (COI) before the job if you'd like to verify.

Do you offer recurring service plans?+

Yes — annual or semi-annual house washing comes with a 15% discount and priority scheduling. Most clients pair it with driveway cleaning twice a year for a meaningful curb-appeal lift.
