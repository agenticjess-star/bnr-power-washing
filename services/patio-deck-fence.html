01The problem

Why wood and stone need different methods

A pressure-washer on cedar or pressure-treated pine fuzzes the grain — even at moderate PSI. The result is wood that looks fine wet but feels rough and uneven once dry, and absorbs new staining faster. Soft wash on wood lifts mildew without damaging the fiber, and a wood-brightener step restores the natural tone. Flagstone, travertine, brick patios, and stamped concrete each have different absorption rates and react to chemistry differently — using one method for everything is how good outdoor spaces get ruined.

02The method

How we treat each outdoor surface

Different surface, different method. We never use one tool for everything — that's how good outdoor surfaces get damaged.

STEP 01

Surface Inventory

Walk the outdoor space — identify wood (deck, pergola, fence), stone (flagstone, travertine, brick), and concrete (stamped, plain). Each gets its own method.

STEP 02

Wood — Soft Wash + Brightener

Biodegradable cleaner at low pressure on wood surfaces. Dwell, gentle rinse, then a wood-brightener pass to restore natural tone. No grain damage, no fuzzing.

STEP 03

Stone — Surface Cleaner + Hand-Detail

Industrial surface cleaner on flagstone & travertine at controlled PSI. Hand-wand on grout lines, edges, and around plant beds. Mineral treatment on iron-stained stone.

STEP 04

Final Pass

Rinse the entire space, sweep off debris, walk-through with you. Outdoor furniture is moved off and replaced as part of the service.

05FAQ

What people ask, straight answers.

Can you clean my wood deck without damaging it?+

Yes — soft wash, never pressure wash, on natural wood. Pressure on cedar or pine fuzzes the grain. Soft wash + brightener restores the natural tone without the damage.

Will the deck need to be re-sealed after cleaning?+

Cleaning resets the surface — most clients re-seal within 2–4 weeks of cleaning to lock in the restored tone. We can recommend trusted refinishers if you'd like.

Do you clean fences?+

Yes — wood, vinyl, and metal. Wood fences get the same soft-wash treatment as decks. Vinyl gets a mild solution + low pressure. Wrought iron gets degreaser + rinse.

Is your method safe for my outdoor plants?+

Yes — biodegradable solution, pre-rinse, post-rinse. We've never had a plant-damage claim. Tarps and misting where landscaping is dense.

Do you do pergolas and outdoor structures?+

Yes — soft wash on wood pergolas, gazebos, and trellises. We work around any electrical or lighting that's installed.

Can you do a deck and fence in the same visit?+

Yes — most clients bundle. Doing both at once saves a setup fee and gives you a 10% discount on the bundled total.
