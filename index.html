03The right method

Soft wash, or pressure wash.

We never use one method for every surface. The wrong choice strips paint, damages siding, or fails to lift the grime. Here's how we decide.

Soft Wash

Low pressure. Gentle solutions.

Low-pressure spray + biodegradable cleaning solutions — gently lifts dirt, algae, and mildew without forcing water into seams or stripping paint. The right choice for anything delicate.

  • Painted siding & stucco
  • Asphalt & tile roofs
  • Wood decks & fencing
  • Vinyl & aluminum
  • Soft stone & brick veneer
vs
Pressure Wash

High pressure. Direct impact.

A focused, high-pressure water jet — no chemicals needed. The right tool for tough, baked-in stains on hard surfaces. We control PSI by surface type so we never go heavier than needed.

  • Concrete driveways & sidewalks
  • Brick & hardscape
  • Dumpster pads & parking lots
  • Oil & rust stains
  • Patios & stone walkways

07Frequently asked

Quick answers.

How does the AI Quote Tool work?
Snap a photo of your driveway, home, deck, or storefront. Our AI estimates square footage, identifies the surface, recommends soft-wash or pressure-wash, and gives you a price range — in 60 seconds. Final price is confirmed at on-site walk-through. No surprises.
What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?
Soft washing uses low-pressure water with biodegradable cleaning solutions to safely remove dirt, mold, and mildew — ideal for siding, roofs, and other delicate surfaces. Pressure washing uses high-pressure water for tough stains on concrete, brick, or other hard surfaces. We use the right method for each surface — never one-size-fits-all.
How often should I have my home or driveway cleaned?
Most Houston homeowners schedule exterior cleaning once or twice a year. High-traffic driveways and commercial properties may benefit from quarterly visits.
Do you use eco-friendly cleaning products?
Yes — every solution we use is biodegradable and safe for landscaping, pets, and people.
Can you clean multi-story buildings or windows?
Yes. We're equipped with professional long-reach tools and safety systems to clean windows and exteriors of multi-story homes and commercial buildings. Fully insured for height work.
Do you serve commercial properties?
Absolutely. Storefronts, restaurants, multi-family complexes, dumpster pads, parking lots, HOA common areas, and recurring maintenance contracts. After-hours and weekend service available.
